📦Shoebox

A second brain for your physical life.

Scan a bill, a letter, a medical record, a certificate. Shoebox reads it, works out what it is, files it into an archive you own, and lets you ask questions about any of it — with the actual document cited back to you.

Download for macOS Apple silicon · signed & notarized · 2026.8.10

How it works

Drop it in

Point your scanner at a watched folder, or drag files into the app. Shoebox picks them up on its own.

It reads

Several OCR engines run over each page — including Apple's own Vision engine, on your Mac — and their results are fused.

It files

Each document is sorted into an organized folder tree that doubles as an Obsidian vault, with a companion note.

You ask

Search across everything, or just ask — like "what do I have to respond to?" Answers cite the documents they came from.

What you get

An archive, not a silo

Your documents stay as ordinary files in ordinary folders. The archive is a standard Obsidian vault — open it in Obsidian, Finder, or anything else.

Search that understands

Hybrid search combines meaning-based and keyword matching, so both "that insurance thing from March" and an exact policy number find the same page.

Chat with citations

Ask questions in plain language. Every answer points back at the source document, so you can check it yourself.

A review queue

When Shoebox isn't confident about a document, it puts it in front of you instead of guessing and filing it wrong.

Where your data lives

  • On your Mac. Documents, the search index, and the database all stay local. There is no Shoebox account and no Shoebox server.
  • Originals are never touched. Files are never overwritten or deleted. Every move is recorded and can be undone.
  • Nothing is invented. When a field can't be read, it's left empty rather than guessed at.
  • The cloud is used for one thing. Understanding a document calls a language model through OpenRouter, using a key you enter yourself in Settings. That's the only part that leaves your machine.

Requirements

macOS Apple silicon (M-series). No Intel build is published.
Signing Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings.
Model access An OpenRouter API key, entered in Settings on first run. Shoebox does not ship with one.
Updates Built in. Shoebox checks for new versions and updates itself.

Installing

Download the .zip, unzip it, and drag Shoebox.app into your Applications folder. Open it and follow the setup, which will ask for your OpenRouter key and where you'd like your archive to live.
